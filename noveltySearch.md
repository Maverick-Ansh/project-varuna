## 0. How this search was done (and how honest it is)

This is a *prior-art / novelty* search, not a legal freedom-to-operate opinion. Five parallel research agents swept five sub-domains — (1) flood digital twins &amp; differentiable hydraulic models, (2) AI/GNN flood prediction &amp; flood-aware routing, (3) urban drainage optimisation &amp; managed aquifer recharge, (4) SAR flood validation, DEM uncertainty, citizen science &amp; night-lights, and (5) patents. Every URL below was returned by live web/patent search on 2026-07-23 and the load-bearing items were fetched and verified. Where a page was paywalled or auto-fetch was blocked (anti-bot), the entry is the canonical DOI/publisher landing page and is flagged. **No citations or patent numbers were fabricated.** Full raw links are in the [Sources appendix](#8-sources--links-full-urls).

**Honest caveats up front.** (a) The *closest single work to Varuna's differentiable core — "Inunda" (arXiv, July 2026) — is essentially concurrent, not something Varuna predates.* (b) Several Varuna ingredients (the Bates-2010 solver, FiLM, HAND/TWI indices, VIIRS Black Marble night-lights, ASR/MAR) are well-established and are cited here as *methods/baselines*, **not** claimed as novel. (c) Varuna's headline metrics (CSI ≈ 0.033, 80.9 % Patna cut, ~5 % vs ~52 % co-location) are its own results; no external work reports those exact diagnostics, which supports novelty but means there is no third-party corroboration of the numbers themselves.

## 1. What *we* (Project Varuna) have done — the novelty anchors

Nine contributions, each stated as a testable claim. Sections 2–3 test each against the field.

| # | Varuna component | The specific claim to defend |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | **Free-tier satellite→twin→dashboard pipeline** | An end-to-end *differentiable* 2-D local-inertial shallow-water flood twin (Bates 2010 scheme in PyTorch) built per-city from **only free global data** (FABDEM, ESA WorldCover, JRC water, Sentinel-1), distilled into a U-Net emulator for millisecond what-ifs, and served as a live public dashboard — the whole build+serve on **free compute** (Colab/Kaggle T4, CPU serve), ~15–25 min/city. |
| V2 | **Honest SAR validation + DEM-uncertainty diagnosis** | Against 8 Sentinel-1 masks the twin scores a *negative* CSI ≈ 0.033–0.041, statistically indistinguishable from TWI/HAND; a ±1 m spatially-correlated DEM ensemble explains *why* — only ~5 % of flooded area co-locates on flat deltaic terrain vs ~52 % in hills. A **terrain-data-regime diagnosis of when no topographic router can co-locate flat-city ponding.** |
| V3 | **Street-graph "spiderweb" drainage, benefit re-simulated** | Storm drains routed on the real OSM road graph via one reverse multi-source Dijkstra from **flood-safe outfalls (never the river)**, ~40 inlets at basin lows, carved descending beds, then **re-simulated** to *measure* a 73–89 % street-flood cut at better cost/m³ than sparse canals. |
| V4 | **FloodGNN — self-supervised street-graph GNN** | Edge-conditioned message-passing GNN over the OSM street graph, **FiLM-conditioned on rainfall** (one model, continuous storm range), trained **only on labels the twin generates** (zero flood ground-truth); per-street risk in a 4 ms pass (~60× faster than raster), driving a flood-safe evacuation-routing endpoint, with **zero-shot flat↔hilly cross-city transfer** (AUC 0.72–0.85). |
| V5 | **Buildability-constrained phased detention siting** | Detention containers sited only on physically-available ground (building footprints &amp; permanent water excluded, under-street allowed), each sized to its local pooled depth, flood-cut **re-measured**, delivered as a 10/20/40 %-cut phased, costed ladder; top sites hit each city's notorious flood spots with zero tuning. |
| V6 | **Flood-to-drought / aquifer-recharge coupling** | A single free-data pipeline that couples **urban flood reduction with managed aquifer recharge** — matching pooled water to a depleted aquifer via a Recharge Suitability Index — framed to India's Atal Bhujal Yojana &amp; CPHEEO norms; V3 "Bhujal" adds metered recharge plans for Karnataka towns. |
| V7 | **Reward-gated citizen-report online learning** | Rate-limited citizen depth-band reports → nightly emulator fine-tuning behind an **auditable reward gate** (band-tolerant loss + simulation-replay anchor against the all-wet failure + held-out-by-day ≥5 % improvement + ≥70 % bootstrap win-rate); every accept/hold decision written to a public learning log. |
| V8 | **Reproducibility-hazard finding (seeded collapse)** | Fixed-seed data generation silently made network init bit-identical, *deterministically* collapsing the softplus emulator head to "never flood" on a half-ocean tile (validation RMSE frozen to the last digit); a detect-and-reseed guard turned the worst tile into the best emulator. A documented **"fixed seeds make failures reproducible too"** result with a frozen-metric tell. |
| V9 | **Grounded multilingual advisories + VIIRS outage overlay** | English/Hindi/Marathi advisories generated from *only* the system's own numbers (strict-JSON, deterministic fallback), plus a VIIRS Black Marble night-radiance-drop overlay flagging candidate power outages (with a "unknown ≠ dark" monsoon cloud-mask caveat). |

## 4. Overall assessment

**No single system or patent was found that combines Varuna's stack** — a differentiable Bates-2010 PyTorch twin **+** U-Net emulator **+** free global open satellite data **+** self-supervised street-graph GNN with routing &amp; transfer **+** re-simulation-measured street-graph drainage **+** buildability-constrained detention **+** flood-to-aquifer-recharge coupling **+** reward-gated citizen online learning — served free-tier for data-scarce Indian cities. Each *ingredient* has strong prior art (catalogued above); the **assembly, and four specific mechanisms, are where novelty concentrates**:

1. **DEM-ensemble co-location diagnosis (V2)** — a genuinely novel, honest-negative contribution.
2. **Re-simulation-measured street-graph drainage with flood-safe non-river outfalls (V3)** — a design + evaluation loop not found in the drainage-optimisation literature or patents.
3. **FloodGNN's five-ingredient combination (V4)** — no located work co-occupies street-graph + FiLM-rainfall + twin-self-supervision + routing + zero-shot transfer.
4. **Reward-gated, audited citizen online-learning for a flood emulator (V7)** — first-of-kind; nearest analog is in nuclear surrogate modelling.

**The one thing to foreground honestly:** *Inunda (arXiv, July 2026)* is a near-concurrent, near-identical differentiable Bates-2010-in-PyTorch core. It must be cited as **related work you distinguish** (emulator + open-data + hosted free-tier twin + the whole planning/GNN/recharge/citizen stack), **not** as something Varuna predates. Do **not** claim novelty on: the Bates-2010 scheme, FiLM, HAND/TWI indices, VIIRS Black Marble, or ASR/MAR as concepts — cite them as methods/baselines.
